# Тиждень 31 — Точні стрибки

**Дні 211–217**

## Мета
Стрибок = look → move → land, а не blind throw.

---

## День 211 — Silent landing

LH C.
Look at upper G.
Move hand and **land without sound**.

Check accuracy.

20 times.

Then G→C.

---

## День 212 — Play after landing

Same drill:
look → move → land → play.

No rush.

10 C→G.
10 A→E.
10 F→C.

### Goal
Accuracy before rhythm.

---

## День 213 — Jumps in pulse

Metronome 45 BPM.

Beat 1 low root.
Beat 3 upper chord.

Start with C only.

Then Am/F/G.

---

## День 214 — Eyes leave early

In a 4-note pattern, once current note is secure, eyes move toward next jump target.

Record hands/eyes if possible from side.

### Goal
Eyes are planning, not reacting.

---

## День 215 — Random targets

Write:
C, G, A, F, D, E.

Random pair.
Low → high.

20 jumps.

Score:
hit / near / miss.

Goal 16+ hit/near.

---

## День 216 — Jumps inside music

Take Dream Piece-like texture.

Isolate exact 1 bar with jump.

Loop:
5 slow silent landings.
5 with sound.
3 in context.

---

## День 217 — Jump Check

10 random jumps.

Target:
9/10 usable landings at slow tempo.

Then 60 sec music with jumps.

### Score
- eyes;
- accuracy;
- rhythm;
- relaxed move;
- recovery.
