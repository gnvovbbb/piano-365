# Тиждень 30 — Витривалість без напруження

**Дні 204–210**

## Мета
Підготувати руки до довгих repeating patterns без тренування болю.

---

## День 204 — Tension map

Грай ostinato 45 sec.

Кожні 10 sec mentally scan:
- jaw;
- shoulders;
- elbow;
- forearm;
- wrist;
- fingers.

Де накопичується tension?

Запиши.

---

## День 205 — Work/rest intervals

4 cycles:
45 sec play.
15 sec rest.

Tempo moderate.

### Rest
Руки реально off keys.
Breath.
Shake gently.

---

## День 206 — 90 seconds

One continuous pattern 90 sec.

Goal:
same quality start/end.

If quality drops at 60 sec:
next time stop at 55 and build gradually.

---

## День 207 — 2 minutes with dynamics

2 min:
p → mp → mf → p.

No f today.

Keep energy economical.

---

## День 208 — Recovery technique

After 60 sec:
stop on planned bar line.
Hands relax 10 sec.
Restart from checkpoint.

Practice restarting calmly.

Useful if performance tension appears.

---

## День 209 — 3-minute Dream Texture

Use 2–4 chords.
3 minutes maximum.

Not speed.
Not maximum loudness.

### Record
Watch shoulders on video.

---

## День 210 — Cinematic Technique Assessment

Use [ASSESSMENTS.md](../ASSESSMENTS.md).

Check:
- 2 min ostinato;
- wide arpeggio;
- crescendo without rushing;
- 3 min no pain.

### If any pain
Do not score through it.
Stop and review technique.
