# Тиждень 29 — Пульс під великим crescendo

**Дні 197–203**

## Мета
Створювати cinematic build без прискорення.

---

## День 197 — Loudness ≠ tempo

Один chord C major.
8 attacks:
p→f.

Метроном 60.

Натискай в той самий момент click.

### Record
Check if later louder notes arrive early.

---

## День 198 — Crescendo over 8 bars

LH ostinato C/Am.

Bars 1–2 p.
3–4 mp.
5–6 mf.
7–8 f.

No RH.

### Goal
4 distinguishable zones.

---

## День 199 — Crescendo with melody

Same build.
RH long melody.

### Voicing
Melody should grow too, but still above accompaniment.

### Warning
Don't slam top notes.

---

## День 200 — Density as dynamics

Instead of only hitting harder:
- p: simple root/fifth;
- mp: full pattern;
- mf: add octave;
- f: thicker voicing if comfortable.

Cinematic crescendo can come from texture, not brute force.

---

## День 201 — Crescendo + pedal

As texture grows, pedal can blur more easily.

Record 30 sec.

If muddy:
- shorter pedal;
- cleaner harmony change;
- reduce inner notes.

---

## День 202 — Build and release

16 bars:
1–8 crescendo.
9 climax.
10–16 diminuendo.

Tempo fixed.

### Ear
Can you point to exact climax?

---

## День 203 — Dynamic Architecture Check

Record 60–90 sec.

Criteria:
- stable tempo;
- clear p/mp/mf/f;
- melody audible;
- climax not at bar 2;
- release after climax.
