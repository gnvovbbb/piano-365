# Тиждень 32 — Педаль у густій фактурі

**Дні 218–224**

## Мета
Зберегти resonance без harmonic mud, коли нот стає більше.

---

## День 218 — No pedal baseline

Take dense 4-bar texture.

Play completely dry.

If it doesn't work dry:
pedal cannot fix it.

### Fix
Notes/rhythm/fingering first.

---

## День 219 — Full change pedal

One harmony per bar.

Chord attack → pedal.
Next chord → release/renew.

Record.

Listen only to boundaries.

---

## День 220 — Shorter pedal

Same phrase.

Version A:
long pedal.

Version B:
shorter/partial-feeling pedal changes.

You don't need advanced half-pedal precision yet.
Just learn that pedal duration can vary.

---

## День 221 — Pedal and bass

Low register muds quickly.

Play low root/octave with less pedal.
Compare to middle register.

### Goal
Pedal depends on register, not fixed formula.

---

## День 222 — Pedal and crescendo

As texture grows:
possibly shorten pedal slightly.

Record p→f.

Listen:
does climax stay clear?

---

## День 223 — Pedal map

On your Dream Piece / cinematic project mark:
- harmony changes;
- places to clear pedal;
- places with longer resonance;
- dry articulation.

Use symbols in legal score or own notes.

---

## День 224 — Pedal Check

60–90 sec dense texture.

Score 0–2:
- harmonic clarity;
- change timing;
- low register;
- crescendo clarity;
- foot control.
